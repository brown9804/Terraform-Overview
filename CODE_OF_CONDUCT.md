# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

This learning community welcomes people with different experience levels,
backgrounds, identities, and perspectives. Participate with patience,
respect, and a focus on helping others learn.

## Expected conduct

- Give constructive technical feedback and explain your reasoning.
- Ask for clarification without belittling a learner.
- Credit others' work and respect copyright and privacy.
- Accept corrections and keep discussions relevant.
- Share sanitized reproductions instead of private operational data.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and publication of someone else's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope and moderation

These expectations apply to project issues, discussions, pull requests, and
other spaces representing this project. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to
protect the community. Responses should be proportionate and consider context.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through an
available contact method on their GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive
details or identify affected people in a public issue. If a private contact
method is unavailable, ask a maintainer publicly only for a private reporting
channel, without disclosing the incident. Immediate safety concerns should
be directed to the appropriate local authorities or GitHub's reporting tools.
